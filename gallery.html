Campus Highlights

Take a virtual tour of our beautiful campus. From our modern classrooms to our state-of-the-art facilities, every corner of Acutina School reflects our commitment to providing an inspiring learning environment.

Modern Classrooms - Equipped with interactive technology and comfortable seating for optimal learning.

Science Laboratory - State-of-the-art facilities for hands-on scientific exploration and experimentation.

Digital Library - A vast collection of physical and digital resources for research and learning.

Sports Complex - Olympic-standard facilities including courts, field, and gymnasium.

Cafeteria - Spacious dining area serving nutritious meals in a welcoming atmosphere.

Art Studio - Creative space for painting, sculpture, and various artistic expressions.

Music Room - Professional-grade instruments and soundproof practice areas for music students.

Lush Green Campus - Beautiful outdoor spaces perfect for relaxation and outdoor activities.
